The Wave Forge extension's library (wave_forge_godot, the .so, .dll or .dylib the extension
loads) is built from Wave Forge's own source, under the MIT License in the repository's LICENSE,
and links godot-rust (gdext) 0.5.5: the crates godot, godot-bindings, godot-cell, godot-codegen,
godot-core, godot-ffi and godot-macros.

godot-rust is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0:
https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/

Its source code is available at https://github.com/godot-rust/gdext, tag v0.5.5
(https://github.com/godot-rust/gdext/tree/v0.5.5), and as the same crates' packages on crates.io,
https://crates.io/crates/godot/0.5.5.
